# ADR-0006: Owned Provider Capability Claim Register

**Status:** Proposed — Q-002 required.

## Decision
Every provider/venue/wallet capability claim is owned, source/config-version snapshotted, scope-limited, expiring, revocable, legally disposed and boundary-tested. Claims are per operation/resource/region/environment, not blanket vendor endorsements.

## Consequences
Documentation, marketing and generic APIs do not prove support. Claim loss/expiry blocks capability. The register is a prerequisite to selecting adapters or reporting provider readiness.
